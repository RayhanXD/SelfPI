# SelfPI Design Context

## Audience
Integration owners and eng leads reviewing automated API-break fixes.

## Brand direction
Black/white quiet base with a **dusk horizon** accent (muted sand → rose → lilac → cool sky). Craft inspired by Linear — not a clone. Own identity: Plus Jakarta Sans + IBM Plex Mono; soft rounded controls; white primary CTAs. App mark: box with looping arrows in horizon stroke.

## Tone
Startup-grade product UI. Calm, precise, expensive-feeling. Dense for scanning; open for review. Trust through clarity.

## Canonical docs (always follow)
**`frontend/design/`** — README, PRINCIPLES, TOKENS, COMPONENTS, SCREENS, DO_AND_DONT.

**UI work happens on `frontend/` → http://localhost:5173** (API-wired product). The old `frontend-lab/` prototype is abandoned — do not extend it.
