# Principles

## Bar

This is shipping software for paying engineering teams someday — not an internal admin console, not a hackathon dashboard, not AI-slop purple glow.

**Craft inspired by Linear. Identity is ours.** Steal interaction discipline (density, focus, quiet chrome). Do not copy Inter, indigo washes, or Linear’s exact chrome.

## Feel

| Word | Means |
|------|--------|
| Calm | Near-black canvas, gray hierarchy, no visual panic |
| Precise | Mono for code-shaped data; labels + values, not paragraphs |
| Trustworthy | Layer + confidence always visible when call sites matter |
| Sparse accent | Horizon band appears rarely; status colors do semantic work |

## Rules of taste

1. **Information first.** Engineers scan state under pressure. Prefer tight lists over marketing whitespace — but not crude tables-with-borders everywhere.
2. **One brand accent: the horizon.** Never a second brand color. Status (`ok` / `warn` / `danger` / `info`) is semantic, not brand.
3. **Show reasoning.** Call sites carry `source_layer` + `confidence`. No black boxes.
4. **Hairlines, not cards-on-cards.** Structure with 1px `white/[0.06–0.08]` borders and subtle surface steps. Nested glossy cards are wrong.
5. **Keyboard-friendly.** Lists and explorers support ↑↓ / Enter where it matters; visible focus rings.
6. **Own the type.** Plus Jakarta Sans (UI) + IBM Plex Mono (code). Not Inter. Not Geist.
